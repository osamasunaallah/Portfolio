From the receiving gate
to the database in seconds.

Built at Alfaisal University to fix slow, error-prone receiving: long waits, missing data and inconsistent records.

Front panel 16×2 LCD RC522 RFID Arduino Uno R4 RFID card & tag Enclosure
Arduino Uno R4Main controller
RC522 RFIDReads driver cards
Load cellWeight checks
How it works
  1. Driver scans an RFID cardThe device reads the card at the receiving gate.
  2. Shipment data is pulled upDriver and shipment details appear on the dashboard.
  3. Worker verifies and editsRecords can be checked and corrected before approval.
  4. Approved record is storedThe shipment is saved to the database for tracking.
